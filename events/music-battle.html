Music Battle

Unleash your musical talents in an electrifying battle of tunes and rhythms. Compete solo or in a band and mesmerize the crowd!

Event Description

The Music Battle is a two-day event where participants showcase their vocal or instrumental talents in a competitive setting.

Event Guidelines

  • Participants can perform solo or as a band (maximum 5 members).
  • Auditions will take place on Day 1, and finalists will perform on Day 2.
  • Each act will have a time limit of 5 minutes.
  • All genres of music are allowed.
  • Backing tracks are permitted, but live performances will be given higher preference.
  • Judging criteria include creativity, technical skill, and crowd engagement.
  • Offensive or inappropriate content will lead to disqualification.
  • Decisions by the judges will be final.

Event Timings

Day 1 - Auditions: 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM

Day 2 - Final Battle: 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM

Event Venue

Location: Open-Air Auditorium, TechFest Campus
