Hackathon

Unleash your creativity and build innovative solutions in a thrilling 2-day hackathon. Collaborate, code, and compete to solve real-world problems!

Event Description

The Hackathon is a 2-day coding marathon where participants develop a working prototype of a product, app, or solution. Teams will brainstorm, design, and develop solutions while receiving mentorship and guidance.

Event Guidelines

  • Teams can have 3 to 5 members.
  • Each team must build a working prototype within 2 days.
  • Pre-built solutions or plagiarism will lead to disqualification.
  • All code must be developed during the hackathon.
  • Judging criteria: Innovation, functionality, impact, and presentation.
  • Mentors will be available for guidance.
  • Participants must bring their own laptops.

Event Timings

Day 1: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Day 2: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Final presentations & judging)

Event Venue

Location: Research Center, Block D, TechFest Campus
