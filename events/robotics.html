Robotics Competition

Build, code, and compete! Showcase your robotic engineering skills in an exciting battle of automation and AI.

Event Description

The Robotics Competition is an exciting challenge for robotics enthusiasts. Participants will design, build, and program robots to complete a set of tasks. The competition will test their innovation, technical skills, and ability to solve real-world problems through automation.

Event Guidelines

  • Teams can have 2 to 5 members.
  • Participants must bring their own robot kits and necessary hardware.
  • The competition consists of multiple rounds with increasing difficulty.
  • Robots must be autonomous or remotely controlled, depending on the challenge.
  • Any form of external assistance during the competition is prohibited.
  • Judges' decisions will be final.

Event Timings

Round 1: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Final Round: 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM
